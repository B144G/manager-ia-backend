# Cookie and Tracker Policy

*Version 2026-11-16 · Last updated: 16 November 2026*

## In brief

MABAH uses **no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers**. It uses only **strictly necessary** cookies and storage for operation, plus — **only if you accept it** — an anonymous visit counter. You express your choice in the banner displayed on your first visit; **refusing is as easy as accepting**, and you can change it at any time via "Manage my cookies" (at the bottom of every page).

## What we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mia_token` | Session cookie, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax` | Keep you logged in to your account (signed token linked to a session that you can close) | Up to 30 days, or until logout | Not required (strictly necessary) |
| `mia_admin` | `HttpOnly` session cookie | Administrator access (reserved for the team) | 12 hours | Not required |
| Interface preferences (language, theme, open section) | Browser local storage | Remember your settings | Until you clear them | Not required |
| Consent choice (version of the text, date, categories) | Browser local storage | Remember your choice and not ask you again | 12 months | Not required |
| Visit counter | **No cookie**: a server call that sends the page visited, the origin of the link (UTM/referring site) and the device type; **neither an IP address nor an identifier** is recorded | Measure audience, improve the site | Data aggregated by day, deleted after 13 months | **Yes**: sent only if you accept "audience measurement" |

## Managing your choices

- Click **"Manage my cookies"** to reopen the banner.
- You can also delete cookies from your browser settings; you will then be logged out.
- If you are logged in, your choice is also recorded in your account, with its date and the version of the text (Privacy → Consents).

## Updates

If we add a non-necessary tracker, we will ask for your agreement again before activating it.
